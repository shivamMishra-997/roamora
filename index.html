Questions, answered.

Does an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. A booking is confirmed only after the team has verified availability, accepted the booking, and completed the stated payment and confirmation process.

Are the prices fixed?

They are indicative estimates. Dates, season, hotel category, group size and supplier rates all change the final figure, which you receive in a written quotation before you pay.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Your preferred destination, travel dates, group size and approximate budget. That is enough for the team to reply with a relevant proposal.

What do packages usually include?

Double-sharing accommodation, breakfast, road transfers as specified and basic trip coordination. Flights, long-distance train tickets, most lunches and dinners, entry fees and optional activities are excluded unless listed.

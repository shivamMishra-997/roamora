Who we are

Roamora is a destination and tour-package guide for India: where to go, how long to stay and what a trip roughly costs.

Every destination and package here is a sample for planning. The team verifies availability, hotels, transport and suppliers before sending a written quotation, and a booking is confirmed only after you approve it and payment is verified. An enquiry on its own never confirms a booking.
